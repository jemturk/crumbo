Crumbo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Crumbo is a cookie-jar themed chat app that a parent sets up so their child can message and call friends in a private, supervised space. This policy explains what information Crumbo collects, how it's used, and the choices parents have. A child does not create their own account — every profile is created and managed by a parent from the Parents Area.

01 Information We Collect

From a parent, when they register:

  • Email address
  • Password — stored securely; Crumbo does not store or have access to a parent's password in plain text

For each child profile a parent creates:

  • A display name chosen by the parent
  • An automatically generated "Cookie Code" (e.g. CRUM-123-456), used to pair a child's profile with buddies from other families. A child never logs in with it — a parent activates a child's profile on a specific device from the Parents Area, and only a parent can do that.
  • An avatar — chosen from a set of emoji, not a photo
  • Which features a parent has enabled or paused for that child (chat, voice calls, video calls, sending photos, sending drawings)

Content created while using Crumbo:

  • Text messages sent between paired children
  • Photos and drawings sent between paired children
  • Records of calls placed — who called whom, when, and how long, not a recording of the call itself
  • Which other child profiles a child is paired with ("buddies")

Automatically collected technical information:

  • A push notification token, used only to alert a device about a new message or incoming call
  • Whether the app is running on a physical device, used only to decide whether push notifications can be registered

We do not collect a child's real name (only whatever display name the parent enters), address, phone number, precise location, or a photo of the child (an avatar is a chosen emoji).

02 How We Use Information

We use the information above only to operate Crumbo's core functionality:

  • Creating and pairing child profiles
  • Delivering messages, photos, drawings, and calls between paired children
  • Enforcing whatever restrictions a parent has set for a specific child
  • Letting a parent review chat and call history for their own children from the Parents Area
  • Sending push notifications for new messages or incoming calls
  • Verifying a parent's email address and enabling password resets for their own account

We do not use any information collected through Crumbo for advertising. Crumbo does not sell or rent information to third parties, and does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

03 Parental Controls

Every child profile is created and owned by a parent through the Parents Area. From there, a parent can at any time:

  • Pause or re-enable chat, voice calls, video calls, sending photos, and sending drawings — independently, for each child
  • Review a child's message history and call log with each of their paired buddies
  • Remove a buddy pairing
  • Deactivate a child's profile on a device (for example, if the device is lost) — access is revoked immediately, and reactivating the profile anywhere always requires signing back into the Parents Area, never just presenting the Cookie Code
  • Delete a child's profile entirely

04 How Information Is Stored and Shared

Crumbo uses the following service providers to operate. Each processes information only as necessary to provide their specific service, and each has their own privacy policy:

ProviderRole
Supabase, Inc. Database, authentication, and file storage for messages, photos, drawings, and account information. Privacy policy
Agora, Inc. Real-time infrastructure carrying audio/video for voice and video calls. Call audio/video is transmitted live and is not recorded or stored by Crumbo. Privacy policy
Resend Delivers transactional emails to parents (verifying an email address, resetting a password). Never receives information about a child. Privacy policy
Expo / Google Delivers push notifications to a device.

We do not share information with any other third party, and we do not sell information to anyone.

05 Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as a parent's account and a child's profile remain active. A parent can delete a child's profile at any time from the Parents Area, which removes it from the parent's account and from the device. To request complete deletion of a child's historical messages or media from our servers, a parent can contact us using the address below.

A parent can also request deletion of their own account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

06 Children's Privacy

Crumbo is intended to be used by children only under the direct setup and ongoing supervision of a parent or guardian. A child cannot create a Crumbo profile on their own — every profile is created by a parent, who controls what it can do and can review its activity or delete it at any time. We do not knowingly allow a child to interact with Crumbo except through a profile a parent has created for them.

If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with information outside of this parent-managed process, please contact us below so we can address it.

07 Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information Crumbo stores. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
